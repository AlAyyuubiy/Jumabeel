LCC Curriculum

What You'll Learn

A comprehensive, practical curriculum designed to take you from complete beginner to confident crocheter.

Measurement & Pattern Reading

Learn the fundamentals of crochet measurements and how to read and follow written patterns independently.

Basic to Advanced Stitches

Master all essential crochet stitches — from chain and single crochet all the way to complex pattern stitches.

Baby Wears & Kiddies Items

Hands-on practicals creating adorable baby wears, sets and accessories using professional techniques.

Crochet Bags & Accessories

Design and create beautiful crochet bags — totes, crossbody, bespoke designs and more from scratch.

Adult Wears & Hijabs

Create wearable adult crochet pieces including basic wears, granny totes and alluring crochet hijabs.

Skill Monetization

Learn how to price your work, photograph your pieces and market your handmade items to start earning.
