About Our Resin Art

What is Resin Art?

Resin art is the process of mixing and pouring epoxy resin — a clear, glossy material — into moulds or onto surfaces to create beautiful, long-lasting decorative pieces.

At Jumabeel Creations, every resin piece is handpoured with care, precision and creativity. From choosing the colours to the final polish — it is all done by Khashiah with intentional artistry.
