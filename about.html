The Brain Behind the Brand

Temitope Aṣabi Khashiyah — Known as Jumabeel

My names are Temitope Aṣabi Khashiyah — born and bred in Iwo, Osun State, but currently married away from home. In the creative world, I am known as Jumabeel — a faceless yet deeply present creative Muslimah behind the brand, Jumabeel Creations.

I am a creative Muslimah striving for Jannah, a student of knowledge, a Public Health Specialist by profession and a multi-disciplinary craft expert who brings a wealth of experience across a diverse range of crafts — from paper crafts, henna art, to resin art and beaded jewelry — with more focus on Crochet.

Known for my exceptional eye for detail and commitment to excellence, I am not only a creator but also a seasoned online tutor and mentor who has impacted many queens through paid and free courses.

What Jumabeel Creations Does

Creativity with Hook, Yarn, Henna & Resin

"I specialise in turning balls of yarn into extraordinary with my gifted hands, beautifying my gender through henna services, fostering and building love with personalised and gifted Resin products. My yarn artistry spans crochet baby wears and bags, to tapestry work for personal home use and business purpose — just for queens like you."

I am dedicated to helping Muslimahs and creative minds refine their techniques, develop new skills, and elevate the quality of their ideas — most especially handmade creations. Every stitch, every henna stroke, and every resin pour is done with intention, love and excellence.

It's nice having you all here — and I can't wait to grab the hook and pull up the loop together with you!

Who We Are

Vision, Mission & Core Values

Our Vision

To be a top and leading global crochet brand offering impact, quality handmade pieces, materials, value and creative space for Muslimahs and mamas who value uniqueness and originality in craft — while proving that you don't have to compromise your values and deen to make impact.

Our Mission

To build a brand that serves, inspires, empowers, fosters warmth and love through crafting adorable handmade products — baby wears and bespoke bags — that are not only beautiful but meaningful and halal for Muslimahs who love uniqueness and those willing to embrace their creative journey.

Core Values

  • We honour the deen in everything we do
  • We value quality over quantity
  • We create with intention — not just for trends, nor compromising the deen
  • Impact and value over mere sales
  • We value honest and halal dealings

"At Jumabeel Creations, it's not just about what we sell — it is why we create. We're still evolving, still growing, but always guided by Allah's light. Follow along, explore our handmade world, or DM to begin your creative journey today."
