Keep It Beautiful

Crochet Care Guide

Follow these simple tips to keep your Jumabeel handmade pieces looking their best for longer.

🫧

Hand Wash Only

Use cold or lukewarm water with mild soap. Avoid machine washing to protect the fibres and shape.

🌬️

Air Dry Flat

Lay flat on a clean surface to dry. Never wring or tumble dry — it distorts the shape of your piece.

🚫

No Bleach

Avoid bleach or harsh chemicals. They weaken the yarn fibres and fade the colour quickly.

📦

Store Properly

Store in a cool, dry place. Fold gently or hang — avoid cramming into tight spaces to maintain shape.
